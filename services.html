BIO REME - MORE SUSTAINABLE

Bio Reme One is a specially formulated blend of highly active beneficial microorganisms designed for the biological treatment and waste management systems, such as wastedumps, landfill sites, compost yards and many more.

The advanced microbial consortium accelerates landfill waste treatment by enhancing the natural decomposition of biodegradable waste. Such micro organisms produce powerful enzymes that break down complex organic matter, resulting in faster organic waste decomposition, improved wasted stabilization, and significant volume reduction.

Bio Reme One also provides effective odor control by reducing the generation of gases, which usually comes with foul smell, in landfill facilities.

Additionally, the reduction of putresicble organic matter helps minimize the breeding conditions for pests, providing effective fly and insect control around the landfill facilities.

CORE FEATURES

BioReme One contains a specialized consortium of naturally occuring beneficial microorganisms that produce powerful enzymes such as cellulase, protease, amlylase and lipase. These enzymes break down complex organic materials, such as food waste, plant matter, proteins, fats, oils, cellulose and other biodegrable compounds into simpler organic matter.
